## Executive Summary
This project explores Customer Relationship Management (CRM) systems using AI tools. The goal was to understand how CRM systems work, compare different products, analyze open-source solutions, and design a basic CRM architecture. AI was used as a research assistant to help structure and support the findings.

## CRM Research Findings
CRM systems help organizations manage customer relationships, improve sales processes, and support marketing and customer service. Common modules include contacts, leads, opportunities, tasks, and reporting. Modern CRM platforms often use cloud-based systems and automation to improve efficiency.

---

## CRM Product Comparisons
Commercial CRM systems like Salesforce, HubSpot, and Zoho CRM are widely used in businesses due to their strong features and scalability. Open-source options like EspoCRM, SuiteCRM, and Odoo CRM provide flexible and cost-effective alternatives for smaller or customizable solutions.

---

## Open Source CRM Evaluation
EspoCRM was explored using its online demo. It provides core CRM features such as contacts, leads, and opportunities. The system is easy to use and well-structured, but some advanced features like reporting and analytics are limited in the demo version.

---

## CRM Architecture Proposal
A basic CRM system can be built using:
- Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Bootstrap, jQuery  
- Backend: PHP  
- Database: MySQL  

The system includes modules such as authentication, contacts, leads, opportunities, tasks, and reporting. It follows a standard three-tier architecture: frontend, backend, and database.
